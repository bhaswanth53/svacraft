07 // THE SEALED GATE

No server.
No audience.

Svacraft has no accounts, no backend, no analytics and no cloud. Your hunter lives in a local SQLite database on your phone. The only person who can see your progress is the one who earned it.

Local by design

Every session, title, note and challenge stays on-device.

Zero telemetry

No tracking SDKs, no sign-in walls, no data broker handshake.

Your backup, your call

Export the full database to JSON. Restore it with validation.

Note Because the database is local, uninstalling the app removes it. Export a backup if you plan to switch devices.
