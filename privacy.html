1. Introduction

Svacraft is a fitness and personal progression application designed to help users track workouts, monitor their progress, manage fitness goals, and build consistent habits through a gamified experience.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. Svacraft follows a local-first approach, meaning application data is stored locally on your device rather than on a developer-operated cloud server.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Svacraft.

2. Information We Collect

Svacraft does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

The application stores information locally on your device, which may include:

  • Workout records: Exercise history, workout sessions, repetitions, and training activities.
  • Progress information: Fitness attributes, levels, achievements, goals, and personal progression.
  • Application preferences: Settings and preferences configured within the application.

This information is stored using SQLite, a local database technology.

We do not operate a backend server that collects or stores your personal workout records or application progress.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information stored within Svacraft is used to provide the application’s functionality, including:

  • Recording and managing workout activities.
  • Tracking fitness progress and training history.
  • Managing personal goals and achievements.
  • Maintaining progression levels and statistics.
  • Saving application preferences.

Your locally stored information is processed within the application to provide these features.

4. Data Storage and Security

Svacraft uses SQLite for local data storage.

Your application records remain on your device and are not transmitted to a developer-operated cloud server.

We do not have direct access to your locally stored workout records, progression information, or application preferences.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any backups containing application data.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Svacraft does not currently integrate advertising networks, analytics platforms, or third-party data collection services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal workout records or application progress with third parties.

Svacraft is developed using React Native and the Expo framework. These technologies are used to build and operate the application.

Google Play may independently process information associated with application distribution, security, payments, and platform services under Google’s own privacy policies.

If additional third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your application data remains stored locally on your device until you delete it.

You can remove your stored information through any data-reset or deletion functionality provided within the application.

Uninstalling Svacraft generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to Android backup and device settings.

We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of your personal application records.

7. Children’s Privacy

Svacraft is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner inconsistent with this policy, please contact us.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct, or delete information.

Since Svacraft stores application records locally and does not maintain user accounts or server-side personal records, users can generally manage or delete their application data directly on their devices.

For privacy-related questions or concerns, you can contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

Any revised version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL with an updated revision date.

We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
